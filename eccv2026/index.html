About me

Building human-centered visual intelligence

I am a computer vision and machine learning researcher with a PhD in Systems Engineering from École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), Montréal. My research focuses on human-centered visual intelligence, multimodal learning, vision-language models, facial dynamics, and personalized model adaptation under real-world distribution shifts.

This work introduces an efficient Action-Unit-guided CLIP adaptation method for fine-grained video emotion recognition, focusing on interpretable facial dynamics and personalized video understanding.
