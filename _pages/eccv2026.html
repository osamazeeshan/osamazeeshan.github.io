About me

Computer vision and machine learning researcher with a PhD in Systems Engineering from École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), Montréal. My research focuses on human-centered visual intelligence, multimodal learning, vision-language models, and personalized model adaptation under real-world distribution shifts.

Main conference

ECCV 2026 Main Conference Paper

CLIP-AUTT: Test-Time Personalization with Action Unit Prompting for Fine-Grained Video Emotion Recognition

This work introduces an efficient Action-Unit-guided CLIP adaptation method for fine-grained video emotion recognition, focusing on interpretable facial dynamics and personalized video understanding.
